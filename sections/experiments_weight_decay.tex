\paragraph{Experiment focus.}
This subsection reports the results of the weight-decay sweep defined in
\cref{sec:method:wd}, evaluated under the shared protocol of
\cref{sec:exp:training,sec:exp:eval}. The only variable that changes is
the AdamW decoupled weight-decay coefficient $\lambda$.

\paragraph{Aggregate numbers.}
\Cref{tab:wd} reports best validation accuracy, final test accuracy,
the train--val gap at the best-val epoch, and wall-clock training
time for each weight-decay setting.

\paragraph{Train--val gap.}
\Cref{fig:wd-gap} plots the train--val accuracy gap as a function of
training epoch for each $\lambda$. A flat or shrinking gap at large
$\lambda$ is direct evidence that weight decay is constraining
memorization rather than just trading capacity for regularization.

\paragraph{Results summary.}
The aggregate numbers in \cref{tab:wd} place all five settings within a
narrow accuracy range. The highest validation accuracy is obtained at
$\lambda=10^{-1}$ with $84.56\%$, while the highest test accuracy is
obtained at $\lambda=0$ with $84.75\%$. The near-baseline setting
$\lambda=10^{-2}$ reaches a similar test accuracy of $84.74\%$. In
terms of overfitting proxy, the smallest train--val gap at the
best-validation epoch is also observed at $\lambda=10^{-1}$, whereas the
wall-clock training times remain essentially identical across the sweep
at approximately 31--32 minutes per run. The interpretation of these
patterns is deferred to the later analysis section.

\paragraph{Limitations.}
\label{sec:wd:limitations}
These runs use a single seed per configuration, so sub-$0.5$ point
differences should be interpreted cautiously because they may fall
within run-to-run noise. In addition, the study fine-tunes from an
ImageNet-pretrained checkpoint rather than training from scratch, so the
conclusions do not necessarily transfer to the longer, more strongly
regularized training regime often used for DeiT-style recipes
\citep{touvron2021deit}. The small absolute spread across neighboring
$\lambda$ values is therefore itself a result: within this controlled
30-epoch protocol, weight decay is not a dominant factor.
