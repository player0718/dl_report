\begin{abstract}

This paper studies regularization for Vision Transformer fine-tuning in a small-data setting. Using ViT-Tiny/16 on CIFAR-100, we build a unified baseline and compare several methods under the same training protocol, including weight decay, dropout-based methods, data augmentation, and label smoothing with early stopping. We also evaluate layer-wise learning rate decay (LLRD) with staged fine-tuning as an optimization strategy. Our results show that different methods contribute at different levels: weight decay gives only small gains, dropout-based methods improve generalization more clearly, and data augmentation is one of the most effective regularization families. Among all tested settings, LLRD with staged fine-tuning achieves the best overall result, improving both validation and test accuracy over the clean baseline. These results suggest that, for pretrained ViTs on CIFAR-100, optimization strategy and input-level regularization are more important than simple parameter penalties alone.
